# My Awards - Agent View

The Awards section is where you can view all the recognition you've received over time. This serves as your personal achievement gallery, showcasing your performance milestones and professional accomplishments.

## Awards Overview

Your awards represent recognition for excellent performance, skill development, and contribution to team success. Each award reflects specific achievements in your role and professional growth.

![Awards Dashboard](../img/screenshots/agent_view/awards/awards-overview.png)

## Viewing Your Awards

### Awards Table

All your awards are displayed in an organized table format showing:

| Column                  | Description                                                 |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Award Name**          | Title of the recognition received                           |
| **Category**            | Type of achievement (Performance, Training, Teamwork, etc.) |
| **Date Received**       | When the award was granted                                  |
| **Awarded By**          | Team lead or supervisor who granted the recognition         |
| **Achievement Details** | Specific accomplishment that earned the award               |
| **Certificate Status**  | Whether a certificate is available for download             |
| **Actions**             | Options to view details or download certificates            |

## Searching and Filtering Awards

### Quick Search

Use the search bar to quickly find specific awards by:

- Award name or title
- Achievement category
- Keywords related to the accomplishment
- Date ranges

![Award Search](../img/screenshots/agent_view/awards/awards-overview.png)

### Date Range Filter

Filter awards by selecting a custom date range:

1. Click on the **Date Range** selector
2. Choose from preset options:
   - This Week
   - This Month
   - Last Month
   - This Quarter
   - This Year
3. Or select custom start and end dates
4. Click **Apply** to filter results

### Category Filters

Filter awards by category to view specific types of recognition:

- Performance awards only
- Learning achievements
- Team collaboration recognitions
- Special milestone awards

### Sort Options

Sort your awards by:

- **Date Received** (newest first or oldest first)
- **Award Name** (alphabetical order)
- **Category** (grouped by type)
- **Awarding Authority** (by supervisor or team lead)

## Award Details

### Viewing Award Information

Click **View Award** to open detailed information including:

- **Complete Award Description**: Full details of the achievement
- **Performance Metrics**: Specific data that earned the recognition
- **Achievement Context**: Circumstances surrounding the award
- **Feedback Comments**: Personal notes from your supervisor
- **Related Goals**: Connection to your performance objectives

![Award Details](../img/screenshots/agent_view/awards/awards-detailed-view.png)

### Award Certificates

#### Downloading Certificates

For awards that include certificates:

1. Click **View Award** to open the award details
2. Click **Download Certificate** button
3. The certificate will download as a PDF file
4. Save the file for your personal records

#### Certificate Features

![Certificate Features](../img/screenshots/agent_view/awards/award-template.png)

Your certificates include:

- Official company branding and logo
- Your name and employee details
- Specific achievement description
- Date of recognition
- Digital signature from awarding authority
- Unique certificate ID for verification

#### Uses for Certificates

- **Personal Portfolio**: Add to your professional portfolio
- **Performance Reviews**: Include in self-assessment documents
- **Career Development**: Use in job applications or promotions
- **Personal Records**: Keep for your own achievement tracking

## Frequently Asked Questions

### About Awards

**Q: How often are awards given?**
A: Awards are given based on performance and achievements, not on a fixed schedule. Some are monthly (like Employee of the Month), while others are given as specific accomplishments are achieved.

**Q: Who can give awards?**
A: Awards are typically given by team leads, supervisors, managers, and department heads based on observed performance and achievements.

**Q: Can I nominate myself for an award?**
A: While you cannot directly award yourself, you can highlight your achievements during performance reviews and one-on-one meetings with your supervisor.

### About Certificates

**Q: Are all awards accompanied by certificates?**
A: Most formal awards include downloadable certificates. Some informal recognitions may not have certificates but are still recorded in your awards history.

**Q: How long are certificates valid?**
A: Certificates do not expire and remain valid indefinitely as recognition of your achievement at that time.

### Technical Support

**Q: What if I can't download my certificate?**
A: Try refreshing the page and clearing your browser cache. If the issue persists, contact IT support or your team lead.

**Q: Can I request a replacement certificate?**
A: Yes, contact your supervisor or HR department if you need a replacement certificate for any reason.
