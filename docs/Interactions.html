My Interactions (Agent View)

Review your call recordings and chat interactions with detailed analysis and performance metrics

Overview​

The Interactions section provides detailed insights into your customer communications across both voice calls and chat sessions. This helps you:

  • Review conversation quality and customer satisfaction
  • Identify patterns in customer inquiries
  • Track your performance metrics over time
  • Learn from successful interactions

Calls​

Monitor and analyze your voice call interactions with customers.

Recent Calls Performance​

View your most recent call interactions with key performance indicators:

Detailed Call View​

Click on any call to access detailed analysis. The detailed view is shown below

  • Full Transcript: Complete conversation text with timestamps
  • Quality Scorecard: Detailed evaluation against company standards
  • Summary: A summary the details of the call (interaction)

Chats​

Analyze your text-based customer interactions and support conversations.

Recent Chats Performance​

View your most recent chat interactions:

Detailed Chat View​

Click on any chat session to see:

  • Summary: Key highlights and overview of the conversation
  • Performance Scorecard: Quality evaluation and response time metrics
  • Chat Details: Customer journey and context before starting the chat
  • Full Transcript: Complete conversation history between you and the customer

Sort​

Sort your interactions to organize them by different criteria such as date, score, or agent name for easier analysis.

Filter​

Use advanced filtering options to narrow down your interactions by specific time periods, categories, or performance metrics.

Export​

Export your interaction data to external formats for further analysis, reporting, or record-keeping purposes.
