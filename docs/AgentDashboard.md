The Agent Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of your individual performance metrics, enabling you to monitor your progress, identify areas for improvement, and track trends over time. This powerful personal analytics tool gives you the insights needed to understand your performance position and guide your professional development journey.

## Overview

Your agent dashboard provides a comprehensive view of your individual performance metrics and the average agent performance in your team, department, or organisation. Everything is designed to help you understand your current standing and identify areas for growth across different categories. This personalised view enables you to take ownership of your performance and make informed decisions about your development priorities.

![Agent Dashboard](../img/screenshots/agent_view/dashboard/dashboard-overview.png)

## Dashboard Metrics Explained

### Category Scores

Track your individual score in multiple categories versus your team score to understand your relative performance position. This assists in your ability to identify areas of improvement and recognise your strengths across different performance dimensions.

**Understanding Category Scores:**
- **Individual Performance**: Your personal score in each category based on recent evaluations
- **Team Average**: The average score across all agents in your team for comparison
- **Performance Gap**: The difference between your score and the team average
- **Trend Indicators**: Visual cues showing whether your performance is improving, declining, or stable

**Category Types:**
- **Customer Service**: How well you handle customer interactions and resolve issues
- **Technical Skills**: Your proficiency with systems, tools, and technical knowledge
- **Compliance**: Adherence to company policies, procedures, and regulatory requirements
- **Communication**: Effectiveness in verbal and written communication with customers
- **Productivity**: Efficiency in handling interactions and meeting performance targets

**Score Interpretation:**
- **Above Average (Green)**: You're performing better than your team average
- **At Average (Yellow)**: Your performance aligns with team expectations
- **Below Average (Red)**: You may need additional support or training in this area

#### Auto Fails

- **Auto-Fails**: Auto Fails represent critical errors that automatically fail an evaluation. This percentage shows what portion of your calls had at least one auto-fail criterion, helping you identify serious performance issues that require immediate attention.

**Understanding Auto Fail Impact:**
- **Critical Nature**: Auto fails indicate violations of essential procedures or policies
- **Immediate Action Required**: These issues typically need urgent intervention
- **Training Opportunities**: Auto fails often highlight specific areas needing additional training
- **Compliance Focus**: Many auto fails relate to regulatory or policy compliance requirements

**Common Auto Fail Categories:**
- **Security Violations**: Unauthorised disclosure of sensitive information
- **Compliance Breaches**: Failure to follow mandatory procedures
- **Customer Harm**: Actions that could negatively impact customer experience
- **System Misuse**: Improper use of tools or platforms
- **Documentation Failures**: Missing or incorrect required documentation

**Reducing Auto Fails:**
- **Review Procedures**: Regularly review company policies and procedures
- **Seek Clarification**: Ask questions when procedures are unclear
- **Use Available Resources**: Access training materials and reference guides
- **Practice Scenarios**: Work through common situations to build confidence

#### Performance Charts

View your performance trends over a specified date range with interactive charts that help you visualise your progress and identify patterns. Use the **Date range** filter to zone in on specific dates you are interested in to analyse:

- **Average Agent Performance trends**: How your team's overall performance is changing over time
- **Your Individual Agent Performance**: Your personal performance trajectory and improvement patterns

![Agent Dashboard](../img/screenshots/agent_view/dashboard/dashboard-charts.png)

**Chart Analysis Features:**
- **Trend Lines**: Visual representation of performance changes over time
- **Comparison Views**: Side-by-side comparison of your performance vs. team average
- **Period Selection**: Choose different time periods for detailed analysis
- **Performance Markers**: Identify specific events or changes that affected performance

**Interpreting Performance Trends:**
- **Upward Trends**: Indicate improvement and skill development
- **Downward Trends**: May signal areas needing attention or support
- **Stable Performance**: Consistent performance that may need enhancement
- **Seasonal Patterns**: Recurring variations that help with planning

## Dashboard Navigation and Features

### Date Range Selection

Use the date range filter to analyse your performance over specific time periods:

**Filter Options:**
- **Quick Filters**: Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last quarter, Current month
- **Custom Range**: Select specific start and end dates for detailed analysis
- **Real-time Updates**: All metrics update automatically when you change the date range

**Best Practices for Date Selection:**
- **Short-term (7-14 days)**: For recent performance review and immediate insights
- **Medium-term (30-90 days)**: For trend analysis and pattern identification
- **Long-term (3-12 months)**: For strategic planning and goal setting

### Metric Details and Drill-Down

Click on any metric to access detailed information and deeper analysis
