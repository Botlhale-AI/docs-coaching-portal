The Admin section serves as your account management center, including both communications and settings management. - **Notifications**: Your in-app communication center for staying informed

## Notifications

The Notifications tab acts as your in-app communication center where you can view messages, updates, and alerts sent through the system. This ensures you never miss critical updates or important communications from your team, helping you stay informed and responsive to organizational needs.

![Notifications](../img/screenshots/agent_view/admin/notifications.png)

**Notification Types:**
- **Course Assignments**: New training courses assigned to you
- **Award Notifications**: Recognition and achievement announcements
- **Deadline Reminders**: Important dates and completion requirements

**Notification Management:**
- **Read/Unread Status**: Track which notifications you've reviewed
- **Action Items**: Notifications requiring your response or action
- **Archive Options**: Organize and manage your notification history

**Best Practices:**
- **Regular Check-ins**: Review notifications daily to stay current
- **Action Tracking**: Address any notifications requiring your response
- **Organization**: Archive or delete old notifications to maintain clarity
- **Priority Setting**: Focus on high-priority notifications first

## Settings

The Settings tab allows you to view and manage your account information, ensuring your account remains secure and up to date with current details. This section provides comprehensive control over your account configuration and security preferences.

### Account Information

![Account](../img/screenshots/agent_view/admin/admin-account-info.jpeg)

View your basic account information and ensure all details are current and accurate:

- **Full Name**: Your complete name as it appears in the system
- **Email**: Your primary email address for communications and login
- **Current Organisation**: Your assigned organization within the platform
- **Current Team**: Your current assigned team and reporting structure

**Account Information Management:**
- **Verification**: Ensure all information is accurate and current
- **Updates**: Contact your administrator if information needs updating
- **Consistency**: Maintain consistency across all platform areas
- **Access Control**: Understand how your information is used and protected

**Information Security:**
- **Data Protection**: Your personal information is protected according to company policies
- **Access Limits**: Only authorized personnel can view your account details
- **Privacy Controls**: You control what information is shared and displayed
- **Audit Trail**: All account changes are logged for security purposes

### Password Management

![Account Settings](../img/screenshots/agent_view/admin/admin.png)

Keep your account secure with robust password controls and security best practices:

**Change Password**

Follow these steps to update your password securely:

1. **Enter Current Password**: Provide your existing password for verification
2. **Create New Password**: Generate a strong password following these requirements:
   - Minimum 8 characters for basic security
   - Include uppercase and lowercase letters for complexity
   - Include at least one number for additional security
   - Include at least one special character for maximum protection
3. **Confirm New Password**: Re-enter your new password to prevent errors
4. **Save Changes**: Click **Save** to apply the new password immediately

**Password Creation Guidelines:**
- **Length**: Longer passwords are more secure (12+ characters recommended)
- **Complexity**: Mix different character types for enhanced security
- **Uniqueness**: Avoid using passwords from other accounts or services
- **Memorability**: Choose something you can remember without writing down
- **Regular Updates**: Change passwords periodically for ongoing security

**Password Security Tips**

Implement these security practices to protect your account:

- **Unique Passwords**: Use a different password for each account and service
- **Password Manager**: Consider using a reputable password manager for secure storage
- **Regular Updates**: Update your password every 90 days as recommended
- **Confidentiality**: Never share your password with colleagues or others
- **Secure Logout**: Always log out from shared computers and public devices
- **Two-Factor Authentication**: Enable additional security measures when available

**Advanced Security Measures:**
- **Multi-Factor Authentication**: Add an extra layer of security beyond passwords
- **Security Questions**: Set up backup verification methods
- **Login Notifications**: Enable alerts for account access from new devices
- **Session Management**: Monitor and control active login sessions

## Account Security Best Practices

### Password Strength Guidelines

**Strong Password Characteristics:**
- **Length**: Minimum 12 characters for optimal security
- **Complexity**: Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
- **Randomness**: Avoid predictable patterns or personal information
- **Uniqueness**: Never reuse passwords across multiple accounts

**Password Examples:**
- **Good**: `K9#mN2$pL8@vR5` (random, complex, unique)
- **Better**: `Blue$ky2024!Secure` (memorable but complex)
- **Avoid**: `password123`, `123456`, or personal information



## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Login Problems

**Issue**: Unable to log in with correct password
**Solutions:**
- Check that Caps Lock is not enabled
- Verify you're using the correct email address
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try using a different browser
- Contact your administrator if the problem persists

**Issue**: Account locked or suspended
**Solutions:**
- Wait 15-30 minutes before attempting to log in again
- Contact your team lead or administrator for assistance
- Verify your account status with IT support
- Follow any instructions provided in lockout messages

### Password Reset Issues

**Issue**: Password reset email not received
**Solutions:**
- Check your spam or junk folder
- Verify your email address is correct in the system
- Wait 10-15 minutes for email delivery
- Contact support if email doesn't arrive within 30 minutes

**Issue**: Password reset link expired
**Solutions:**
- Request a new password reset immediately
- Use the reset link within the specified time limit
- Contact support if you continue to have issues

### Notification Problems

**Issue**: Not receiving expected notifications
**Solutions:**
- Check your notification settings and preferences
- Verify your email address is current and accessible
- Review spam filters and email settings
- Contact your administrator to verify notification settings
