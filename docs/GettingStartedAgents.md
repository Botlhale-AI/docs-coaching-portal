Welcome to the Vela Coaching Portal! This platform is your dedicated space for professional growth, helping you track your performance, review interactions, and manage your development. This guide will walk you through the main sections of the portal.

---

## 1. The Dashboard: Your Performance at a Glance

After logging in, you'll land on your **Dashboard**. Think of this as your central hub. It provides a clear, visual summary of your performance and compares it to your team or organization's average.

To get started, use the **date picker** to select a time frame. The dashboard will instantly update to show your scores, trends, and comparisons for that period, helping you identify your strengths and areas for growth.



---

## 2. The Interactions Page: Review Your Calls and Chats

Navigate to the **Interactions** section to find a complete history of your customer conversations.

### Finding and Filtering Interactions

All your calls and chats for the selected date range appear in a sortable table. You can use filters to quickly find a specific interaction. You can filter by:
* Call ID
* Date
* Handle Time
* Silent Time
* Topic
* Agent Score

You can also **export** this list as a CSV file for offline analysis.

### Diving into Call Details

Clicking on any call opens the **Call Details** view. Here, you can listen to the **audio playback** and review the **Transcrop**—a synced transcript that highlights the text as the audio plays. If the original call was not in English, you can view an English translation when available.

### Understanding the Smart Detector

Within the Call Details view, you'll find the **Smart Detector**. It has two key tabs:
* **Summary Tab:** Gives a quick overview of the main topics and themes discussed in the call.
* **Scorecard Tab:** Shows the evaluation questions and your results for the interaction. It clearly breaks down what you did well and where you have opportunities to improve.



---

## 3. The Courses Page: Access Your Training

The **Courses** section is your personal learning library. Here you can find all training modules assigned to you, organized by their status:
* **Not Started**
* **In Progress**
* **Assigned**
* **Completed**

For completed courses, you can always revisit the material, download documents, and retake quizzes to refresh your knowledge. Your quiz scores are displayed immediately after completion.

---

## 4. The Awards Page: Track Your Achievements

Head to the **Awards** section to see all the recognitions and achievements you've earned. You can filter your awards by date or type to reflect on your accomplishments and stay motivated. 🏆

---

## 5. The Settings Page: Manage Your Account

Finally, the **Settings** section is where you can manage your profile. Here, you can view your account information and update your security credentials to keep your account safe.

This concludes the tour of the Vela Coaching Portal—a space designed to support your growth and success as an agent.
