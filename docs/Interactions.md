As an agent, view and analyze your call recordings and chat interactions to understand your performance and identify areas for improvement. This comprehensive guide helps you leverage interaction data to enhance your customer service skills and professional development.

## Overview

The Interactions section provides detailed insights into your customer communications across both voice calls and chat sessions. This powerful analysis tool helps you:

- Review conversation quality and customer satisfaction metrics
- Identify patterns in customer inquiries and interaction types
- Track your performance metrics over time with trend analysis
- Learn from successful interactions and apply best practices
- Understand customer sentiment and emotional responses
- Develop targeted improvement strategies based on data insights

### Call Analytics

#### Performance Metrics

Track your call performance with these comprehensive key indicators that provide detailed insights into your effectiveness:

- **Handle Time**: Total duration of customer interactions from start to finish
  - **Target Ranges**: Optimal handle times vary by call type and complexity
  - **Efficiency Analysis**: Balance between thoroughness and efficiency
  - **Trend Monitoring**: Track changes in handle time over time

- **Agent Score**: Overall performance rating based on evaluation criteria
  - **Scoring Components**: Communication, problem-solving, compliance, and professionalism
  - **Benchmark Comparison**: Compare against team and organizational standards
  - **Improvement Tracking**: Monitor score progression and identify trends

- **Silent Time**: Periods of inactivity or silence during calls
  - **Acceptable Thresholds**: Normal silent time varies by call type
  - **Efficiency Indicators**: Excessive silence may indicate process issues
  - **Training Opportunities**: Identify areas where you can improve efficiency

#### Common Call Topics

Understanding the topics you handle most frequently helps you prepare and develop expertise in specific areas. These are examples of common call topics:

1. **Billing Inquiries**: Questions about charges, payment methods, and account balances
2. **Technical Support**: Assistance with product functionality and troubleshooting
3. **Account Management**: Profile updates, security settings, and account modifications
4. **Product Information**: Details about features, pricing, and availability
5. **Complaints**: Customer concerns and issue resolution processes

**Topic Analysis Benefits:**
- **Skill Development**: Focus training on frequently encountered topics
- **Resource Preparation**: Ensure you have access to relevant information
- **Process Improvement**: Identify opportunities to streamline common interactions
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Share insights with colleagues about effective approaches

#### Call Quality Insights

Review detailed feedback on your call handling to understand strengths and areas for development:

- **Strengths**: Identify what you're doing well and should continue
  - Communication clarity and professionalism
  - Problem-solving effectiveness
  - Customer rapport building
  - Technical knowledge application

- **Areas for Improvement**: Specific opportunities for development
  - Process adherence and compliance
  - Time management and efficiency
  - Technical skill enhancement
  - Customer service techniques

- **Coaching Recommendations**: Actionable suggestions for improvement
  - Specific training courses or resources
  - Practice opportunities and scenarios
  - Mentoring or shadowing experiences
  - Self-study recommendations

### Detailed Call View

Click on any call to access comprehensive analysis that provides deep insights into your performance. The detailed view includes:

- **Full Transcript**: Complete conversation text with timestamps for precise analysis
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Customer emotion tracking throughout the call with mood indicators
- **Topic Detection**: Automatically identified discussion points and key themes
- **Quality Scorecard**: Detailed evaluation against company standards with specific criteria
- **Customer Feedback**: Post-call survey responses and satisfaction ratings (when available)

- **Handle Time**: Total duration from first message to resolution
  - **Response Efficiency**: Balance between thoroughness and speed
  - **Multi-tasking Capability**: Ability to handle multiple conversations
  - **Quality vs Speed**: Maintaining quality while meeting response time targets

- **Response Time**: Time between customer messages and your replies
  - **First Response Time**: Initial reply speed to customer inquiries
  - **Average Response Time**: Typical reply speed throughout conversations
  - **Response Consistency**: Maintaining steady response times across interactions

#### Common Chat Topics

Most frequent chat conversation topics that help you understand your typical workload and prepare accordingly. These are examples of common chat topics:

1. **Order Inquiries**: Questions about order status, tracking, and delivery
2. **Product Support**: Technical assistance and product information
3. **Account Issues**: Login problems, password resets, and account access
4. **Billing Questions**: Payment issues, refunds, and account charges
5. **General Information**: Company policies, hours, and general inquiries



### Chat Quality Insights

Review feedback on your chat support performance to understand your written communication effectiveness:

- **Strengths**: Areas where you excel in chat support
  - Clear and concise written communication
  - Quick and accurate problem resolution
  - Professional tone and approach
  - Effective use of chat features and tools

- **Areas for Improvement**: Opportunities to enhance your chat skills
  - Response time optimization
  - Message clarity and completeness
  - Technical knowledge application
  - Customer engagement techniques

- **Coaching Recommendations**: Specific suggestions for chat improvement
  - Typing speed and accuracy training
  - Chat etiquette and best practices
  - Technical knowledge enhancement
  - Multi-tasking skill development

### Detailed Chat View

Click on any chat session to see comprehensive analysis of your written interactions:

- **Complete Chat Log**: Full conversation history with timestamps and message details
- **Response Time Analysis**: Breakdown of your reply speeds and patterns
- **Customer Journey**: Path taken before initiating chat and interaction flow
- **Resolution Steps**: Actions taken to solve the customer's issue
- **Satisfaction Survey**: Post-chat customer feedback and ratings
